Keywords

Which words on your designs are actually doing the selling, and whether each one is climbing or fading. Use this to decide how to tag the next design — and to spot designs you never tagged at all.

Run Sync Design Tags on the Spoondollar history page to refresh this. Multi-word tags are split into individual words (e.g. "vintage golf sport" counts toward "vintage", "golf", and "sport" separately) so a design's own repeated keyword phrasing doesn't dominate the list without telling you much — ordered here by how many different designs share a word first, then by revenue, so words proven across your catalog surface above one design's own tag list. Totals run higher than your overall revenue, since one design's sale counts toward each of its distinct words. Click a keyword to filter the transaction table to the designs carrying it.

    Swatch conversion

    all-time

    How often ordering a swatch led the same buyer to come back later for a full-size order of that same design — all-time, not affected by the date filter above, since a sample can take months to convert. Only signed-in buyers can be followed this way: Spoonflower anonymizes guest checkouts, so a guest's swatch and their later order can't be tied to the same person. A full-size order placed the same day as the swatch isn't counted, since that's almost always one combined order rather than a sample doing its job.
