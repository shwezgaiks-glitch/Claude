Revenue by tag

Run Sync Design Tags on the Spoondollar history page to refresh this. Multi-word tags are split into individual words (e.g. "vintage golf sport" counts toward "vintage", "golf", and "sport" separately) so a design's own repeated keyword phrasing doesn't dominate the list without telling you much — sorted here by how many different designs share a word first, then by revenue, so words proven across your catalog surface above one design's own tag list. Revenue totals still run higher than your overall revenue, since one design's sale counts toward each of its distinct words. Designs with no tags synced don't contribute any words above — see the list below instead.

    Keyword trends

    Design families

    Designs grouped by how much of their tag vocabulary they share — which tends to recover the sets you published together (one motif in several colorways, or a coordinate collection) without looking at the images. Ranked by the group's combined revenue, so you can see which whole themes earn, not just which individual designs do. Click a group's name to filter the transaction table to it.

    Swatch conversion

    How often ordering a swatch led the same buyer to come back later for a full-size order of that same design — all-time, not affected by the date filter above, since a sample can take months to convert. Only signed-in buyers can be followed this way: Spoonflower anonymizes guest checkouts, so a guest's swatch and their later order can't be tied to the same person. A full-size order placed the same day as the swatch isn't counted, since that's almost always one combined order rather than a sample doing its job.
